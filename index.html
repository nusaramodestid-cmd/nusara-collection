Why Nusara Collection

Bridging Indonesian Craftsmanship with Maldivian Serenity.

Indonesia stands as the world's most vibrant hub of modest fashion innovation — renowned for tailoring that harmonizes contemporary aesthetics, exquisite natural fibers, and timeless modesty.

Yet, living in or vacationing across the Maldives requires clothing that respects warm tropical humidity. Heavy polyester abayas and synthetic fabrics fall short.

Nusara Collection acts as your personal fashion curator. We travel across Indonesia — from design studios in Jakarta to artisanal weaving villages in Bandung, Yogyakarta, and Bali — handpicking silhouettes made strictly from cooling linens, pure silk crepes, and breathable tencel blends.

Four Pillars of Tropical Modesty

Every garment in our collection is rigorously evaluated for island climate performance.

Enzyme-Washed Linen

French flax linen processed in Indonesia with organic enzymes for buttery softness that breathes effortlessly.

Mulberry Silk Crepe

Featherweight natural silk with subtle matte luster that resists tropical humidity cling during seaside dinners.

Tencel Eucalyptus Modal

Rapid moisture-wicking and antibacterial plant-based fibers that stay crisp and cool against sun-kissed skin.

Non-Sheer Modesty

High-density micro-weaves and custom inner bamboo slips ensure 100% opacity under bright atoll sunlight.
